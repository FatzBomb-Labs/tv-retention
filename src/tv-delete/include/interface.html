How a rule decides

  
A rule may set any combination of keep days, keep episodes, and keep seasons. Each condition votes on every episode, and the combine mode settles disagreements.

  

    
Combine modeAn episode is deleted whenEffect

    
      
Earliestevery condition says soKeeps the most. The safe default.

      
Latestevery condition says so, and none was undecidableKeeps the least, without acting on an unknown.

      
Anyany condition says soMost aggressive. Ignores conditions it cannot judge.

Air dates

  
Sonarr's air date is used first, then TMDB if a key is set, then the file's modification time. The source of each date is shown in every preview, so it is clear when a decision rests on a timestamp rather than a real air date.
